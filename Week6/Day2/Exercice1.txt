 Exercise 1 – Compare Instructions

# Before changing the system prompt

I submitted the question:

"What is your refund policy?"

The chatbot could generate a general answer and might invent a refund policy that was not provided.

### After changing the system prompt

I submitted the same question again.

The chatbot gave a shorter and clearer answer. It did not invent a company refund policy and explained that it needed more information.

# Comparison

Before the change, the chatbot was less restricted and could provide unsupported information.

After the change, the chatbot followed the new instructions:
- Short answers.
- Simple language.
- Clarification questions when necessary.
- No invented company policies.

# Conclusion

Changing the system prompt improves the chatbot's response quality and reliability. It helps prevent incorrect information and makes responses clearer and safer.
